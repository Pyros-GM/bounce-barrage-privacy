Privacy Policy for Bounce Barrage

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bounce Barrage (the "App"), an Android game published on Google Play by Eternal-T Games ("we", "us"), handles information when you play.

In short:

  • No account of ours to create. No ads. No analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your progress, coins, items and settings are saved on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your own Google Play Games account (cloud save), so you can carry it to a new phone. We never receive that copy.
  • Online duels pass live game data between you and your opponent through our game servers. The servers keep nothing once the match is over.
  • When you buy coins, our server asks Google Play to confirm the purchase is real before the coins are given. It doesn't store the purchase.
  • The daily streak reminder is off unless you turn it on, and it is created on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • The only thing other players see is the display name you choose.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, and the App does not collect, personal information such as your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID.

Online duels. When you play an online duel (Ranked, Unranked, or a private room with a code), the App connects to our game servers, which pass the following between you and your opponent:

  • A temporary session ID that the server creates for your connection. It is not linked to you or your device and changes every time you connect.
  • Your display name: one you choose, or a random name such as "Player 1234". It is limited to 12 letters, numbers and simple symbols, and your opponent can see it.
  • Your game choices (ball, paddle and block colours, block layout, background and ability).
  • Live gameplay data: paddle positions, ball and block positions, scores and the preset emotes ("GG", "Nice!", "Wow!", "Oops!"). There is no free-text chat.
  • The room code and the game version, so players are matched with others on the same version.

Coin purchase checks. When you buy a coin pack, the App sends our server the pack's product ID and the purchase token that Google Play gives the App. Our server sends these to Google Play (the Google Play Developer API) to confirm that the purchase is real and not already used, then tells the App whether to give the coins. The server does not store the token. Apart from a short line in the server's log (for example, that a check for a coin pack succeeded or failed), nothing about the purchase is kept, and none of it includes payment details.

Server time. For the daily login streak and the daily challenge, the App asks our server for the current date and time. This request carries no personal information.

Like any online service, our servers and their hosting provider receive your device's IP address in order to connect to it. We do not store it or use it to identify you, although the hosting provider may keep standard connection logs for a short time. We also use it briefly to limit how many purchase checks one address can make, to prevent abuse.

Waking the servers. When the App opens, it sends a simple request to our game servers so they are ready if you want to duel. This request carries no personal information.

Feedback. If you contact us using "Send Feedback" or by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

3. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

Cloud save is optional. It uses Google Play Games Services, and it only works if you are signed in to Google Play Games (Google may sign you in automatically if you already use Play Games, and Settings has a "Sign in" button otherwise).

  • The App stores one saved game, containing the data marked * in Section 2, in your Google Play Games account. It is uploaded when something changes, after you buy coins, and when the App goes to the background.
  • When you open the App on another phone signed in to the same account, the App can restore that copy. If both have different progress, it asks which one to keep.
  • The saved game is stored by Google, not by us. We cannot see it, and it never passes through our servers.
  • Signing in gives the App your Google Play Games player profile so it can read and write your saved game. The App does not use your profile for anything else and does not send it to us.

4. Third-party services

  • Render (render.com) hosts our game servers in the United States. It processes connection data, including IP addresses, to run them. See Render's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play distributes the App. Coin pack payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing: we never see your card or payment details. Our server checks each purchase with the Google Play Developer API (Section 1). Cloud save uses Google Play Games Services (Section 3). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Links: the Settings screen links to our Discord server, our website, this policy and your email app. Those services have their own privacy policies.

The App contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

5. How we use information

  • To run online matches: pairing players, passing game data between them, and letting you rejoin after a dropped connection.
  • To show your opponent your display name.
  • To confirm coin purchases with Google Play and give you the coins you bought.
  • To give you the correct daily rewards, using the server's date.
  • To keep a copy of your progress in your Google Play Games account, if you are signed in.
  • To remind you about your streak, only if you turn the daily reminder on.
  • To reply to feedback you send us.

We do not use information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Game servers: match data is kept only in memory while the match is running. It is discarded when the room closes, shortly after both players leave. Purchase checks are answered and not stored, apart from the short log lines mentioned in Section 1, which the hosting provider deletes after a limited time. We keep no database of players, matches or purchases.
  • Your device: saved data stays until you uninstall the App or clear its data (Android Settings > Apps > Bounce Barrage > Storage > Clear data). This deletes your progress, coins and items on that phone, and it cannot be undone.
  • Cloud save: kept in your Google Play Games account until you delete it. You can delete your Play Games data for the App, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games settings or your Google account's data settings.
  • Emails: kept only as long as needed to help you. Ask us at any time and we will delete them.

7. Data security

Online duels use secure WebSocket connections (WSS), and purchase checks and server-time requests use HTTPS, so they are encrypted in transit. Cloud saves are handled by Google Play Games over Google's own secure connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the information we handle to a minimum.

8. Children's privacy

Bounce Barrage is a casual game for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no free-text chat. Players can only exchange a short display name and preset emotes, and we encourage children not to use their real name as their display name. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by email), contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and choices

  • You can play every bot stage and the daily challenge without going online.
  • You can change your display name at any time in Settings or on the Duel screen.
  • Cloud save is optional: you can play without signing in to Google Play Games.
  • The daily reminder is off unless you turn it on in Settings, and you can turn it off again at any time (or block the App's notifications in Android settings).
  • You can set vibration to Off in Settings.
  • You can delete all saved data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it, and delete your cloud save as described in Section 6.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete your personal information. Because we keep almost none, there is usually nothing to send or delete, but you can contact us with any request.
